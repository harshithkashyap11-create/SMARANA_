# Phase 5 demo — Doctor portal

1. Sign in as `deka@example.com` and confirm only assigned patient cards appear, including open flags and last activity.
2. Open Rao and switch between Overview, Metrics, Difficulty, Routine & Medicines, Notes, Alerts, and Report.
3. In Metrics, compare 7, 30, and 90 days; confirm the non-diagnosis notice and that guest sessions are absent.
4. Add a medication with two dose times and confirm two routine entries plus a caregiver information alert.
5. Set, lock, unlock, and cap a game level with a reason; confirm the change history preserves explanations.
6. Assign an exercise and confirm its weekly completion and review date appear.
7. Save a baseline and notes at each visibility level; verify caregiver/patient visibility using their accounts.
8. Leave the doctor session idle and confirm professional automatic logout; verify access tokens expire after 15 minutes.
